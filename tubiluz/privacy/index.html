Tubiluz – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2026 · Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Tubiluz (Android package com.harrylabs.tubiluz, “the game”) handles information. The game is published on Google Play by HarryLabs Games, the developer name of Harenson Henao, an individual developer based in Colombia (“we”, “us”).

1. Summary

  • We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.
  • We do not run our own servers or our own analytics. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google, as a third party, automatically collects certain device data (such as the advertising ID, IP address and device information) to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and diagnose problems.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google’s consent tool (User Messaging Platform) before ads can use your data for personalization.
  • The game is not directed to children under 13.

2. Who is responsible

Data controller / “responsable del tratamiento” for the processing that we decide on: Harenson Henao (HarryLabs Games), Colombia. Contact: harrylabs.games@gmail.com.

For the data that Google collects through its advertising services, Google is an independent controller and its own privacy policy applies (see section 4).

3. Information stored only on your device

The game saves the following in its private storage on your device so that it works as expected. This information is not sent to us or to anyone else by the game:

  • Game progress: highest unlocked level, completed levels, number of random boards solved per difficulty and whether you have seen the tutorial.
  • Settings: music and sound on/off and volume, vibration on/off, colorblind mode and language.
  • A technical log file of the app, used only for troubleshooting on the device.

You can delete this information at any time by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the game. “Reset progress” in the level selector resets your level progress.

4. Advertising – Google AdMob

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google Ireland Limited). An ad may appear after every 3 completed levels, and you can choose to watch a rewarded ad to get extra hints or undos. The tutorial has no ads.

According to Google’s published disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK, the SDK automatically collects and shares the following data for advertising, analytics and fraud-prevention purposes:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of the device.
  • Device and account identifiers: the Android advertising ID, the app set ID and, if applicable, other identifiers related to signed-in accounts on the device.
  • Product interactions: for example app launches, taps on ads and ad video views.
  • Diagnostic information: information about the performance of the app and the SDK, such as app launch time, hang rate, energy usage and crash/diagnostic data.
  • General device information needed to deliver an ad, such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size and type of network connection.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, detect and prevent fraud and abuse, and, only where you have consented or where permitted by law, to personalize ads. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). For details on how Google uses this information and how to control it, see:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses cookies and identifiers in advertising
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK – Google Play data disclosure

Your choices: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on the device). You can also manage your Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

5. Consent (GDPR / UK GDPR / Swiss law and other regions)

When required, the game shows a consent message provided by Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP), certified under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework, before personalized ads are requested. You can accept or refuse, and you can change your choice at any time through “Privacy options” in the game’s Settings. If you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalized or limited ads, which use data such as the IP address and device information for delivery, frequency capping, aggregated measurement and fraud prevention. Where required by other privacy laws (for example certain U.S. state laws), the game may also show the corresponding Google message.

6. Legal bases

  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland (GDPR): your consent for storing or accessing information on your device and for personalized advertising (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); our legitimate interest in financing a free game through non-personalized ads and in preventing fraud and keeping the service secure (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Brazil (LGPD – Lei nº 13.709/2018): consent (Art. 7, I) and legitimate interest (Art. 7, IX), as applicable.
  • Colombia (Ley Estatutaria 1581 de 2012 and its regulations, Decreto 1074 de 2015): your prior, express and informed authorization, given through the consent message and by using the game after being informed by this policy, which is our data processing policy (“política de tratamiento de datos”).

7. International transfers

Google may process data on servers located in different countries, including the United States. Google states that it relies on legal mechanisms such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses and adequacy decisions for these transfers. By using the game you are informed that ad-related data may be processed outside your country.

8. Children

Tubiluz is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has used the game in a way that resulted in data being collected, contact us and we will help you request deletion from Google. In some countries a higher age (up to 16) applies to consent for online services; users below that age should use the game only with the permission of a parent or guardian.

9. Data retention

We do not keep any personal data on our side. The data stored on your device stays there until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the game. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google’s retention policy: policies.google.com/technologies/retention. If you write to us by email, we keep your message only as long as needed to answer and follow up on your request, and then delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, rectify, update, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, to withdraw your consent at any time (without affecting prior processing), to be informed about how your data is used and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, for example:

  • Colombia: Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC) – www.sic.gov.co
  • Brazil: Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (ANPD) – www.gov.br/anpd
  • European Union / EEA: your local data protection authority – list of authorities

Because we do not hold your personal data, most requests about advertising data are handled by Google, through the tools listed in section 4. You can always write to harrylabs.games@gmail.com and we will help you. Under Colombian law we will answer queries (“consultas”) within 10 business days and claims (“reclamos”) within 15 business days, extendable as provided by Ley 1581 de 2012.

11. Device permissions

  • Vibration – optional haptic feedback (can be turned off in Settings).
  • Internet and network state – needed to load ads.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) – added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to read the advertising ID for the purposes described above.

The game does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

12. Security

The game does not transmit your progress or settings. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we choose providers that apply industry-standard protections.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the game or the services it uses change. We will publish the new version on this page with a new “Last updated” date and, if the changes are significant, we will inform you in the game or in the store listing.

14. Contact
